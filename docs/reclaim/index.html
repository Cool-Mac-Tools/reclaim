My Mac

gigabyte actually went.

Reclaim walks your whole data volume and reconciles it — exactly — to the real used space macOS reports. Categories drill in to a full browser where you can filter by size and age, bulk-select, and remove straight to quarantine.

Even "System Data," explained.

That opaque bucket gets itemized into Protected Data, macOS System, System Update Files, Virtual Memory, and residual snapshots — so nothing is a mystery. Big bundles like Photo libraries and apps stay whole, never carved up.

Leftovers from apps
you already deleted.

Uninstalling an app rarely removes everything. Reclaim cross-references what's installed against the data on disk and surfaces the caches, application support, and preferences left behind by apps that are long gone.

Careful by default.

A system-bundle allowlist keeps macOS's own components off the list, and anything still owned by a live recipe is excluded — so you only see genuinely dead data.

Duplicate finder

The same file,
saved five times.

Reclaim groups files by size, then confirms true duplicates with a sampled SHA-256 hash — so it's fast and it's certain. It keeps the newest copy and offers the rest for removal.

Scoped to your stuff.

Duplicate detection is limited to personal categories — photos, movies, music, documents, downloads — so it never offers up the identical files your dev toolchains legitimately keep.

Nothing is deleted.
It's quarantined.

When you clean, items don't vanish — they move to a reversible vault on your Mac with a manifest for one-click restore. Undo anytime, or purge when you're sure. Reclaim honors macOS: it skips anything protected or in use rather than forcing it.

Trust is the product

Built to protect what matters.

Reclaim scans read-only, prefers each tool's own supported removal method, and stops cold at anything macOS protects. Every finding lands in one of five clearly-labeled tiers.

Green — Regenerable

Caches, previews, staging. One-click, after checking the app is closed.

Blue — Reversible

Re-downloadable data and app leftovers. Quarantined with an undo window.

Yellow — History

Chat history, sessions, logs. Explained, then only removed if you choose.

Orange — Personal

Photos, messages, cloud-synced files. Never auto-deleted. Ever.

Red — Protected

System data and sealed assets. Reclaim reports them and leaves them alone.

Why Reclaim?

Other cleaners guess and delete.
Reclaim explains, then lets you decide.

Not another black-box cleaner.

The old apps show a spinning dial and a scary number, then delete things you can't see. Reclaim shows you every finding, tells you what it is and whether it comes back, and never acts until you say so.

App-aware, not path-aware.

Reclaim knows the difference between a rebuildable cache and your actual work — because it understands the tool that made it.

Reversible by default.

Everything goes to a quarantine you can undo. You purge only when you're sure.

It proves the space.

A rescan after every cleanup measures the real free-space delta — no imaginary gigabytes.
