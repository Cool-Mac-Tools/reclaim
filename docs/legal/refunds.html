Refund Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

Reclaim is free to download and to scan. If you subscribe to unlock cleanup and it isn't for you, email support@reclaimac.com within 14 days and we'll refund you in full — no questions asked. Refunds go back to your original payment method via Stripe, our payment processor (typically within 5–10 business days).

Cancelling your subscription

Cancel anytime from the app's Manage Subscription button or the Stripe customer portal. Cancelling stops future charges; cleanup stays unlocked until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew every 6 months. Stripe emails a receipt for each renewal. If you were charged and didn't mean to renew, contact us within 14 days of the charge and we'll refund it.

Optional AI costs are billed by your provider

If you turn on the optional AI explanations, you connect your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google account and are billed directly by that provider for the requests you make. Reclaim never charges you for this, and any billing questions are handled under that provider's own terms.

support@reclaimac.com — we usually reply within one business day.
