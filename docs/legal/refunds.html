Refund Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Reclaim is free

Reclaim is currently free to download and use — there's nothing to pay for, so there's nothing to refund. Every feature is available at no cost.

Optional AI costs are billed by your provider

If you turn on the optional AI explanations, you connect your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google account and are billed directly by that provider for the requests you make. Reclaim never charges you for this, and any billing questions are handled under that provider's own terms.

If a paid plan launches later

If we introduce a paid plan in the future, we'll publish a clear money-back guarantee here before you can buy anything. We'd rather refund a dissatisfied customer than keep an unhappy one.
